Solstice Analytics — Parental Leave Policy
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for paid parental leave upon the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child, regardless of tenure. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week are eligible on a pro-rated basis.

2. Leave Duration
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents (including through adoption or foster placement) receive 8 weeks of paid parental leave. This leave is in addition to any short-term disability benefit that may apply to birth recovery.

3. Timing and Flexibility
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or placement date. Leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks of at least 2 weeks each, with manager and HR approval for split arrangements.

4. Pay and Benefits During Leave
Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary. Health benefits continue uninterrupted during parental leave with no change to employee contribution amounts. PTO and sick leave do not accrue during weeks of unpaid leave but do continue to accrue during paid parental leave weeks.

5. Job Protection
Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed the same or an equivalent role, level, and compensation as before their leave began.

6. Requesting Leave
Employees should notify their manager and People Operations at least 30 days before the expected leave start date where possible (e.g., for births), or as soon as practicable for unplanned placements (e.g., some adoptions or foster placements).

Note: Leave duration figures in Section 2 were updated after this policy's original publication — refer to the most recent Policy Addendum for the current entitlement, which supersedes the numbers above if a conflict exists.
