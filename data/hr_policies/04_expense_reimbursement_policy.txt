Solstice Analytics — Expense and Reimbursement Policy
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: Finance & People Operations

1. Purpose
This policy covers how employees are reimbursed for approved business expenses.

2. Submitting Expenses
Expenses must be submitted through the Expensify system within 30 days of being incurred. Submissions after 30 days may be denied at Finance's discretion. Each expense over 25 USD requires an itemized receipt; a credit card statement alone is not sufficient documentation.

3. Travel Expenses
Airfare should be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours and premium economy for flights over 6 hours, using the corporate travel portal when available. Hotel expenses are reimbursed up to 250 USD per night in standard cities and up to 350 USD per night in high-cost cities (a list of designated high-cost cities is maintained on the internal portal). Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, train) is reimbursable when directly related to business travel.

4. Meals
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to a daily cap of 75 USD, itemized as up to 15 USD for breakfast, 20 USD for lunch, and 40 USD for dinner. Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a client meal, up to 2 drinks per person, and must be itemized separately. Meals for internal team gatherings without a client present are capped at 30 USD per person and require director-level approval.

5. Approval Chain
Expenses under 500 USD require direct manager approval. Expenses between 500 and 2,000 USD require director approval. Expenses above 2,000 USD require VP approval and should be discussed with Finance before being incurred where possible.

6. Non-Reimbursable Items
The company does not reimburse: personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, airline lounge memberships (unless pre-approved for a specific role), spa or gym services, or expenses without valid receipts.

7. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed via payroll within 2 pay cycles of submission approval.
