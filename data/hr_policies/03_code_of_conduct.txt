Solstice Analytics — Code of Conduct and Workplace Behavior
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations & Legal

1. Purpose
This Code of Conduct sets expectations for professional behavior for all employees, contractors, and interns at Solstice Analytics.

2. Respectful Workplace
Solstice Analytics prohibits harassment, discrimination, and retaliation based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, veteran status, or any other legally protected characteristic. This applies to interactions in the office, remote work settings, company events, and online communication using company systems.

3. Reporting a Concern
Employees who experience or witness a violation of this Code should report it to their manager, any People Operations team member, or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline available on the internal portal. Reports may be made confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

4. Investigation Process
People Operations will acknowledge a report within 2 business days and aim to complete an initial investigation within 15 business days. Timelines may extend for complex cases; the reporting employee will be updated on status at least every 2 weeks during an active investigation.

5. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role, including romantic relationships with direct reports. Undisclosed conflicts of interest may result in disciplinary action.

6. Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, accounts, and systems are provided for business use. Limited incidental personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work, violate other policies, or involve illegal activity.

7. Confidential Information
Employees must not disclose confidential business information, customer data, or trade secrets to unauthorized parties, both during employment and after departure, consistent with the confidentiality agreement signed at hire.

8. Disciplinary Process
Violations of this Code may result in a verbal warning, written warning, performance improvement plan, suspension, or termination, depending on severity, as determined by People Operations in consultation with the employee's manager. Severe violations (e.g., harassment, safety threats, theft) may result in immediate termination without prior warning steps.
