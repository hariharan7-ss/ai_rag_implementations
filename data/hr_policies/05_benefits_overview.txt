Solstice Analytics — Benefits Overview
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Health Insurance
Solstice Analytics offers medical, dental, and vision coverage through Meridian Health Group, available to full-time employees starting the first day of the month following their start date (no waiting period beyond that). The company covers 80% of the employee premium and 60% of dependent premiums for the base PPO plan. A high-deductible plan with an HSA option is also available, with the company covering 90% of the employee premium and contributing 500 USD per year to the employee's HSA.

2. Retirement Plan
Employees are eligible to enroll in the 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. Solstice Analytics matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary, and 50% of the next 2% (so a 6% employee contribution receives a 5% company match). Matching contributions vest immediately.

3. Life and Disability Insurance
The company provides basic life insurance at 1x annual salary and short-term disability coverage at no cost to the employee. Additional voluntary life insurance and long-term disability coverage are available for purchase during open enrollment.

4. Wellness Benefit
Employees may expense up to 50 USD per month toward gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps, submitted through the standard expense process referenced in the Expense and Reimbursement Policy.

5. Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses, books, conferences, or certifications relevant to their role, subject to manager approval.

6. Open Enrollment
Open enrollment for health benefits occurs each November for coverage starting the following January 1st. Outside of open enrollment, benefit elections can only be changed following a qualifying life event (marriage, birth/adoption of a child, divorce, loss of other coverage) within 30 days of the event.
