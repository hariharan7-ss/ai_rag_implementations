Solstice Analytics — Performance Review Process
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Review Cycle
Solstice Analytics runs a formal performance review twice per year: a mid-year check-in (June) that is developmental and not tied to compensation, and an annual review (December) that determines ratings, merit increases, and bonus payouts.

2. Rating Scale
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory. Ratings are calibrated across each department by managers and a People Operations partner before being finalized, to reduce inconsistency between managers.

3. Self-Assessment
Employees complete a self-assessment in the performance system prior to each review cycle. The self-assessment is due 2 weeks before the manager's written review is due.

4. Manager Review
Managers write a review covering goal achievement, core competencies, and growth areas, and hold a 1:1 conversation with the employee to discuss the rating before it is finalized in the system.

5. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" or "Unsatisfactory" in the annual review, or who show a sustained pattern of underperformance identified by a manager, may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan. A PIP defines specific goals and a timeline, typically 30 to 60 days, with a formal check-in at the midpoint. Failure to meet PIP goals by the end of the timeline may result in termination.

6. Merit Increases and Bonuses
Merit increase budgets are set annually by Finance and allocated by department based on average rating distribution. Individual merit increases and bonus amounts are communicated to employees within 30 days of the annual review being finalized, typically in late January or early February.

7. Promotions
Promotions are considered during the annual review cycle by default, but may be processed off-cycle with VP approval for exceptional circumstances (e.g., retention risk, significant scope change).
