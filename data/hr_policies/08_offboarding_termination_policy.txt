Solstice Analytics — Offboarding and Termination Policy
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Voluntary Resignation
Employees who resign are asked to provide at least 2 weeks written notice for individual contributor roles and 4 weeks for manager and above roles, submitted to their manager and People Operations. Notice periods are requested but not legally binding except where local employment law requires otherwise.

2. Involuntary Termination
Involuntary termination may occur for performance reasons (typically following an unsuccessful Performance Improvement Plan, per the Performance Review Process policy), policy or Code of Conduct violations, or business reasons such as role elimination. People Operations must review and approve any involuntary termination before it is communicated to the employee.

3. Final Pay
Final paycheck, including accrued and unused PTO (per the Leave and Time Off Policy), is issued according to state or local law, typically within the final pay cycle or immediately upon termination where required by law.

4. Benefits Continuation
Health benefits end on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Employees may be eligible to continue coverage through COBRA (US) or equivalent local programs at their own cost. Information is provided by People Operations within 14 days of the termination date.

5. Equipment and Access
Departing employees must return all company equipment (laptop, badge, peripherals) on or before their last working day. IT will disable system access at end of day on the last working day, or immediately in cases of involuntary termination for cause.

6. Exit Interview
People Operations offers an exit interview to all departing employees to gather feedback. Participation is voluntary.

7. Rehire Eligibility
Employees who resign in good standing with proper notice are generally eligible for rehire. Employees terminated for cause are marked "not eligible for rehire" in the HR system.
