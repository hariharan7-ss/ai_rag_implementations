Solstice Analytics — Leave and Time Off Policy
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Purpose
This policy explains how paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and public holidays work for all full-time employees of Solstice Analytics.

2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. PTO accrual begins on an employee's start date. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise. PTO requests must be submitted in the HR system at least 5 business days in advance for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days. Requests of 1-2 days may be submitted with 24 hours notice, subject to manager approval based on team coverage needs.

3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over year to year and does not need advance notice, but employees should notify their manager before their normal start time on the day of absence. For absences of 3 or more consecutive sick days, a doctor's note may be requested by HR.

4. Public Holidays
Solstice Analytics observes 11 paid public holidays per year. The list of observed holidays is published by People Operations each December for the following year and posted on the internal HR portal.

5. Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted PTO and sick leave may request unpaid leave of up to 10 business days per year with manager and HR approval. Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance except in emergencies.

6. Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 paid bereavement days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 2 paid days for extended family. These days do not count against PTO.

7. Notes on Interaction with Other Policies
Parental leave is governed separately — see the Parental Leave Policy document, not this policy. Remote employees follow the same PTO accrual and request rules as in-office employees; there is no difference in PTO entitlement based on work location.
