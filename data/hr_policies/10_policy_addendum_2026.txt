Solstice Analytics — HR Policy Addendum
Effective: January 1, 2026
Owner: People Operations
Applies to: Parental Leave Policy, Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

This addendum updates specific figures in existing HR policies. Where a figure below conflicts with the original policy document, this addendum controls as of its effective date.

1. Parental Leave Duration Update
Effective January 1, 2026, birthing parents receive 20 weeks of paid parental leave (increased from 16 weeks). Non-birthing parents receive 10 weeks of paid parental leave (increased from 8 weeks). All other provisions of the Parental Leave Policy, including timing, pay level, and job protection, remain unchanged.

2. Remote Work Equipment Stipend Update
Effective January 1, 2026, the one-time home office equipment stipend for remote and hybrid employees increases from 400 USD to 600 USD. The monthly internet stipend increases from 25 USD to 35 USD.

3. No Other Changes
All other terms of the Parental Leave Policy and the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy remain in effect as originally written. This addendum does not modify the Leave and Time Off Policy, Code of Conduct, Expense and Reimbursement Policy, Benefits Overview, Performance Review Process, Offboarding and Termination Policy, or Working Hours and Overtime Policy.
