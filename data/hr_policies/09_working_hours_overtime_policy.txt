Solstice Analytics — Working Hours and Overtime Policy
Effective: January 1, 2025
Owner: People Operations

1. Standard Hours
The standard work week is 40 hours. Exact daily schedules may vary by team as long as core hours (see Remote and Hybrid Work Policy, Section 4) are met.

2. Exempt vs Non-Exempt Classification
Employees are classified as "exempt" or "non-exempt" under applicable wage and hour law, noted in their offer letter and in the HR system. Exempt employees (most salaried professional roles) are not eligible for overtime pay. Non-exempt employees (including hourly and some salaried operational roles) are eligible for overtime pay.

3. Overtime for Non-Exempt Employees
Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a work week. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager whenever possible; unapproved overtime that is actually worked must still be paid, but may result in a conversation about scheduling with the employee's manager.

4. Meal and Rest Breaks
Non-exempt employees are entitled to a 30-minute unpaid meal break for shifts over 6 hours, and two paid 15-minute rest breaks for shifts over 8 hours, consistent with local law.

5. Timekeeping
Non-exempt employees must record hours worked daily in the timekeeping system. Falsifying time records is a Code of Conduct violation and may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

6. On-Call Work
Employees placed on a formal on-call rotation receive an on-call stipend of 100 USD per week on-call, plus overtime pay (if non-exempt) or a flat callout fee of 75 USD (if exempt) for any incident requiring more than 30 minutes of active work outside standard hours.
